## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is one chapter going from storyboard (ネーム) to finished ink, laid on a vertical webtoon strip. It refuses the category default of a hero plus a card grid. Panels are the navigation, and gap length is the timing.

OWN-WORLD: Bright manuscript board (#F5F5F2), G-pen ink (#0D0D0D), non-repro blue guides and trim marks (#A9C8E8, structure only), and one fluorescent spot ink (#FF1F6F) that works as the editor's pencil. Hard black frames at three stroke weights. Tone shows up only at named events. SFX display lettering is used for beats only. Buttons are inked frames, and the pressed state fills with tone.

STORY: See both halves (ML and shipped product) → believe the circled proof → email or open GitHub.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A splash panel bleeding past the trim. AAKSHANT KUMAR as SFX lettering filling roughly 60% of the width. A positioning line under it. Two insets overlap the lower-right corner (finalist; 113/292), each circled in spot ink. Primary CTA 'Read the work' (spot fill) and secondary 'GitHub / Résumé' in the bottom-left frame. A page counter 'p.01/NN' sits in the margin, and a sticky 'Contact' tab stays visible.

FORM: Name to Ink with the Vertical Webtoon Strip pacing mixed in, per the user's steer. Candidate 6 of 7. Seed 18a0c93d. Raises: spot-ink flood at the 2 structural transitions (Session Sleeve); addressable panels with a real page-counter nav (HyperCard); a fixed lattice for spans and gutters (Teletext); event-bound tone and speed lines (Film); every mark pairs shape with color (Color Chord). Signature interaction: panels snap from rough blue-line to ink as they cross 30% of the viewport, driven by scroll position. Under reduced motion this becomes a crossfade.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
